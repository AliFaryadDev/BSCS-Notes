## Introduction

Managerial roles are the different activities and responsibilities performed by managers.

Managerial skills are the abilities required to perform managerial duties effectively.

## Managerial Roles

According to Henry Mintzberg, managerial roles are divided into three main categories.

### 1. Interpersonal Roles

These roles involve relationships and interactions with other people.

**Types:**

- Figurehead: Performs formal and symbolic duties.
- Leader: Guides, motivates, and supervises employees.
- Liaison: Maintains relationships with people inside and outside the organization.

### 2. Informational Roles

These roles involve collecting and sharing information.

**Types:**

- Monitor: Collects information about organizational activities.
- Disseminator: Shares information with employees.
- Spokesperson: Provides information to people outside the organization.

### 3. Decisional Roles

These roles involve making decisions and solving problems.

**Types:**

- Entrepreneur: Introduces new ideas and improvements.
- Disturbance Handler: Solves unexpected problems.
- Resource Allocator: Distributes organizational resources.
- Negotiator: Negotiates with individuals and groups.

## Managerial Skills

According to Robert Katz, managers need three basic skills.

### 1. Technical Skills

Technical skills are the ability to use specific knowledge, methods, and tools to perform tasks.

**Example:** A computer manager understanding programming and software.

### 2. Human Skills

Human skills are the ability to communicate, cooperate, and work effectively with other people.

**Example:** Motivating employees and solving conflicts.

### 3. Conceptual Skills

Conceptual skills are the ability to understand the organization as a whole and analyze complex situations.

**Example:** Developing long-term business strategies.

## Importance of Managerial Skills

- Improve decision-making.
- Increase employee productivity.
- Improve communication.
- Help solve organizational problems.
- Support achievement of goals.

## Conclusion

Managerial roles describe what managers do, while managerial skills describe the abilities they need to perform their responsibilities.
