## Introduction

Every organization establishes goals to determine what it wants to achieve in the future.

Organizational goals provide direction and guide managerial decisions.

## Definition of Organizational Goals

Organizational goals are the desired results or outcomes that an organization aims to achieve within a specific period.

## Types of Organizational Goals

### 1. Strategic Goals

Long-term goals established by top-level management.

**Example:** Becoming a market leader within five years.

### 2. Tactical Goals

Goals established by middle-level management to support strategic goals.

**Example:** Increasing the sales of a department by 15%.

### 3. Operational Goals

Short-term goals related to daily organizational activities.

**Example:** Completing 50 customer orders every day.

## Other Common Types of Goals

### 1. Official Goals

Goals formally stated in an organization's mission or official documents.

### 2. Operative Goals

Goals that describe the actual activities and priorities of an organization.

## Characteristics of Effective Goals

1. Specific: Goals should be clear.
2. Measurable: Progress should be measurable.
3. Achievable: Goals should be realistic.
4. Relevant: Goals should support organizational objectives.
5. Time-Bound: Goals should have a deadline.

These characteristics are commonly known as SMART goals.

## Importance of Organizational Goals

- Provide direction.
- Motivate employees.
- Improve coordination.
- Help measure performance.
- Support decision making.
- Increase organizational effectiveness.

## Conclusion

Organizational goals define the results an organization wants to achieve and help managers direct activities toward success.
