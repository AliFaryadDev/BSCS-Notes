## Introduction

Organizational environment refers to all internal and external factors that influence the activities and performance of an organization.

It is divided into two main types:

1. Internal Environment
2. External Environment

## 1. Internal Environment

The internal environment includes factors within an organization that affect its operations.

**Main Components:**

### Employees
People who work in the organization.

### Management
Managers who make decisions and guide organizational activities.

### Organizational Culture
Shared values, beliefs, and practices of employees.

### Organizational Resources
Resources such as money, equipment, technology, and information.

## 2. External Environment

The external environment includes factors outside an organization that influence its operations.

It is divided into two main categories.

### A. General Environment

The general environment includes broad factors that affect many organizations.

**Main Components:**

1. Economic Factors: Inflation, interest rates, and unemployment.
2. Political Factors: Government policies and political stability.
3. Legal Factors: Laws and regulations.
4. Social Factors: Values, traditions, and lifestyle.
5. Technological Factors: New technologies and innovations.
6. Global Factors: International competition and global markets.

### B. Task Environment

The task environment includes external groups that directly interact with an organization.

**Main Components:**

1. Customers: People who purchase products or services.
2. Competitors: Other organizations offering similar products.
3. Suppliers: Provide raw materials and resources.
4. Regulators: Government agencies that control business activities.
5. Strategic Partners: Organizations that cooperate with the business.

## Difference Between Internal and External Environment

| Internal Environment | External Environment |
|---|---|
| Exists inside the organization. | Exists outside the organization. |
| Includes employees and management. | Includes customers and competitors. |
| Often more directly controllable. | Usually less controllable. |
| Affects internal operations. | Influences organizational decisions and performance. |

## Conclusion

Both internal and external environments influence organizational success. Managers must understand these factors to make effective decisions.
