## Introduction

Managers are individuals who guide, supervise, and coordinate the activities of employees to achieve organizational goals.

Managers can be classified according to their organizational level and functional area.

## Managers Based on Organizational Level

There are three main levels of managers:

### 1. Top-Level Managers

Top-level managers are responsible for the overall direction and performance of an organization.

**Examples:**
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- President
- Managing Director

**Responsibilities:**
- Set organizational goals.
- Develop long-term strategies.
- Make major decisions.
- Coordinate different departments.

### 2. Middle-Level Managers

Middle-level managers connect top-level managers with lower-level managers.

**Examples:**
- Department Manager
- Branch Manager
- Regional Manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Implement organizational plans.
- Manage departments.
- Coordinate lower-level managers.
- Report performance to top management.

### 3. First-Line Managers

First-line managers directly supervise employees who perform daily operational tasks.

**Examples:**
- Supervisor
- Office Manager
- Team Leader

**Responsibilities:**
- Assign daily tasks.
- Supervise employees.
- Monitor performance.
- Solve routine workplace problems.

## Managers Based on Functional Area

Managers may also be classified according to their areas of responsibility.

### 1. Marketing Managers
Manage advertising, sales, and customer relationships.

### 2. Financial Managers
Manage budgets, investments, and financial resources.

### 3. Human Resource Managers
Manage recruitment, training, and employee relations.

### 4. Operations Managers
Manage production and delivery of goods and services.

### 5. Information Managers
Manage information systems and organizational technology.

## Conclusion

Different kinds of managers perform different responsibilities, but all work together to achieve organizational goals.
