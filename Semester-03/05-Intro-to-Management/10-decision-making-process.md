## Introduction

Decision making is an important management activity. Managers make decisions to solve problems and select the best possible alternatives.

## Definition of Decision Making

Decision making is the process of identifying a problem and selecting the best alternative from available options.

## Steps in Decision-Making Process

### 1. Recognizing the Problem

Identifying and understanding the problem that requires a decision.

### 2. Identifying Alternatives

Finding different possible solutions to the problem.

### 3. Evaluating Alternatives

Analyzing the advantages and disadvantages of each alternative.

### 4. Selecting the Best Alternative

Choosing the most suitable solution.

### 5. Implementing the Decision

Putting the selected solution into action.

### 6. Evaluating the Decision

Checking whether the decision has successfully solved the problem.

## Types of Decisions

### 1. Programmed Decisions

Routine decisions made for common and repeated problems.

**Example:** Reordering office supplies when stock becomes low.

### 2. Non-Programmed Decisions

Unique decisions made for unusual or complex problems.

**Example:** Deciding to open a new business branch.

### 3. Strategic Decisions

Major decisions that affect the long-term direction of an organization.

### 4. Tactical Decisions

Decisions made to implement strategic plans.

### 5. Operational Decisions

Daily decisions related to routine organizational activities.

## Importance of Decision Making

- Helps solve organizational problems.
- Supports achievement of goals.
- Improves resource utilization.
- Reduces confusion.
- Helps managers respond to challenges.
- Improves organizational performance.

## Conclusion

Decision making helps managers select suitable solutions and guide organizational activities toward success.
