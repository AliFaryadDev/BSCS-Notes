## Introduction

Planning takes place at different levels of an organization. Each level focuses on different objectives and responsibilities.

## Definition

Levels of planning refer to the different organizational levels at which plans are developed and implemented.

## 1. Strategic Planning

Strategic planning is long-term planning performed mainly by top-level managers.

**Characteristics:**
- Focuses on long-term goals.
- Covers the entire organization.
- Deals with major opportunities and threats.
- Provides overall organizational direction.

**Example:** Expanding a company into another country.

## 2. Tactical Planning

Tactical planning is medium-term planning performed mainly by middle-level managers.

**Characteristics:**
- Supports strategic plans.
- Focuses on specific departments.
- Converts broad strategies into departmental actions.
- Usually covers a shorter period than strategic planning.

**Example:** Preparing a sales strategy for a particular region.

## 3. Operational Planning

Operational planning focuses on short-term and daily activities.

**Characteristics:**
- Deals with routine tasks.
- Focuses on specific activities.
- Guides employees and supervisors.
- Supports tactical plans.

**Example:** Preparing daily employee schedules.

## Difference Between Planning Levels

| Strategic Planning | Tactical Planning | Operational Planning |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term | Medium-term | Short-term |
| Top management | Middle management | First-line management |
| Entire organization | Departments | Daily activities |
| Sets overall direction | Converts strategy into actions | Guides routine work |

## Conclusion

Strategic, tactical, and operational planning work together to ensure that organizational goals are achieved at every level.
