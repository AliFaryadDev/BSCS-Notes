## 1. Introduction

The management process is a set of activities performed by managers to achieve organizational goals.

## 2. Definition of Management Process

The management process is the process of planning, organizing, leading, and controlling resources to achieve organizational goals.

## 3. Main Functions of Management

The management process consists of four main functions:

1. Planning
2. Organizing
3. Leading
4. Controlling

## 4. Planning

Planning means deciding in advance what to do, how to do it, and when to do it to achieve goals.

**Example:** Deciding the targets and schedule for a project.

## 5. Organizing

Organizing means arranging resources and assigning tasks to people to achieve goals.

**Example:** Dividing work among team members.

## 6. Leading

Leading means guiding, motivating, and directing employees to complete their tasks.

**Example:** A manager guides and motivates employees to perform their work.

## 7. Controlling

Controlling means checking performance and comparing it with planned goals to correct mistakes.

**Example:** Checking whether employees have completed their work on time.

## 8. Steps of Management Process

The management process follows four main steps:

1. **Planning:** Decide what needs to be done.
2. **Organizing:** Arrange resources and assign tasks.
3. **Leading:** Guide and motivate people.
4. **Controlling:** Check performance and correct mistakes.

## 9. Conclusion

The management process helps managers achieve organizational goals through planning, organizing, leading, and controlling activities.
